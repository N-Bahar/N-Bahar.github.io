# Nilgoun Bahar, PhD SLP
<p class="byline">/niːl'ɡuːn bæˈhɒːɾ/</p>

<p class="subtitle">Postdoctoral fellow and paediatric speech-language pathologist at the Dyslexia Center at the University of California, San Francisco (UCSF).</p>

I'm fascinated by how children learn to speak and read, and by how the developing brain supports, or struggles with, these skills. My research focuses on the neural and cognitive mechanisms underlying language-based learning disorders. Through this work, I hope to develop biologically informed approaches to early assessment and intervention to help affected children thrive in our chatty, literacy-dependent world. As a clinician, I'm also proud that I am often the one collecting my own data, which gives me unparalleled insight into each child's profile and the population of children I've chosen to work with.
