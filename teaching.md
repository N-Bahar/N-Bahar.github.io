# Teaching & Mentorship

My teaching experience comes from the University of Oxford, where I taught as a tutor within the University's tutorial system. Oxford tutorials are weekly, small-group teaching sessions (typically one to three students) that complement lectures through guided discussion. Tutorials revolve around essays, research papers, or problem sets prepared in advance, with the tutor responsible for planning the session, leading discussion, challenging students' ideas, and providing detailed feedback on their written work.

Between 2022 and 2024, I tutored the following undergraduate courses:

- Introduction to Cognitive Psychology
- Psychology of Language
- Developmental Science
- Neurobiology of Speech & Language

I also worked as a teaching assistant for Cognition Core Practicals and fMRI: Experimental Design & Analysis, where I supported students with experimental design, statistical analyses, coding, and essay writing and assessment.

My current position at UCSF is centered on clinical work and research rather than teaching. I do, however, enjoy mentoring students and currently supervise summer research interns as well as an undergraduate student completing her honour's thesis in our lab.
